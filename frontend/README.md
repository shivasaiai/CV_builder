# CV Builder

## Overview
CV Builder is a versatile and user-friendly tool designed to simplify the creation of professional resumes. The application offers a range of features to help users showcase their skills, experience, and education in an aesthetically pleasing format.

## Features
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Easy navigation for seamless resume creation.
- **Customizable Templates:** Choose from various templates to suit your style.
- **Real-Time Preview:** See changes as you make them with a live preview feature.
- **Download Options:** Export your resume in multiple formats (PDF, Word, etc.).
- **Cloud Save:** Save your resumes to the cloud for easy access from anywhere.

## Usage
1. **Choose a Template:** Start by selecting a resume template that aligns with your personal brand.
2. **Fill in Your Information:** Input your personal details, including education, work experience, and skills.
3. **Customize the Design:** Make adjustments to layout, fonts, and colors to make your resume unique.
4. **Preview:** Use the real-time preview feature to see how your resume looks.
5. **Export:** Download your completed resume in your desired format.
